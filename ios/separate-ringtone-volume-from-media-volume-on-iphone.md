# Separate Ringtone Volume from Media Volume on iPhone

## Problem
* Press "-" button to lower the volume when play media in Weixin(Wechat)
* Ringtone volume is also affected(lower)
* Need to separate ringtone volume from media volume

## Root Cause
* "Change with buttons" is ON in settings

## Solution
Go to Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Toggle Change with Buttons to OFF.
This will enable you to adjust the volume without the side buttons affecting the ringtone and notification volume.

# Send Copies of Group Mails to Group Members' Inboxes

## Problem
* Created Office 365 Group
* Send a test mail to the group
* Members can find the test mail in Outlook > Switch to Group > Select the Group
* But the test mail can not be found in members' Inboxes

## Solution
* Login Office 365 > Admin Portal > Teams and groups > Active teams & groups 
* Select the group > Settings Tab > Email: Send copies of team mails and events to team members' inboxes
